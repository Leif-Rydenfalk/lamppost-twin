What the twin claims — and what it does not

The headline switch time comes from Open-Meteo's irradiance, linearly interpolated between hours (±10 min). The violet marker is the simpler clear-sky + cloud model, scored against Open-Meteo; its spread against the headline is in the panel. The bars are 12 hours either side of now; the 40 and 60 W/m² threshold lines are on the same scale.

  • Open-Meteo is a model, not a sensor on this mast. No observation is claimed.
  • The 40/60 W/m² thresholds and the 8.0 m mast are assumptions.
  • In Betrieb means “not reported broken”, not “lit”.
  • The scene is a schematic of the promenade, kerb, carriageway and lindens, not a survey.
